Hermes Workspace

A private, single-user assistant integration for Google Workspace.

What this is

Hermes Workspace is a personal software project. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it has no users other than its developer. It connects one Google Account — the developer's own — to Google Workspace APIs so that a self-hosted personal assistant can read, search and organise that account's own data on the account holder's behalf.

What it does

  • Reads and searches Gmail to surface what needs attention in a daily personal briefing.
  • Reads calendar events to include the day's schedule in that briefing.
  • Reads and organises files in Drive — including moving, renaming and uploading documents to structured folders.
  • Reads and updates Google Sheets used for personal tracking, and uploads generated reports.
  • Reads and manages Google Tasks.
  • Reads contacts and directory information to resolve names when the account holder asks about a person or organisation.
  • Reads Docs, Slides and Forms content belonging to the account.

Every action is taken at the account holder's request or on a schedule the account holder has configured. Nothing is done on behalf of any other person or organisation.

How it is used

Exclusively for the account holder's own personal productivity: a morning briefing, task tracking, filing and reporting. The integration is not offered to the public, has no third-party users, and is not a commercial product.
